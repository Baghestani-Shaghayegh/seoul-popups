Privacy Policy

MGN RADAR (“Seoul Popups”) · Last updated 7 August 2026

MGN RADAR helps you find pop-up stores and events in Seoul. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address Only if you create an account, or sign in with Google, Kakao, Naver, or Apple To identify your account and let you sign back in
Display name and profile photo Only if you set them Shown to you in the app on your own profile
Approximate location Only while you use the map or nearby features, and only if you grant permission To show pop-ups near you and sort them by distance
Photos you select Only the single image you pick as a profile picture To set your avatar. We never scan or browse your photo library
Saved and visited pop-ups When you tap save or mark a visit To sync your list across your devices
Push notification token Only if you allow notifications To alert you when a saved pop-up is ending soon

You can use most of the app — browsing, searching, and the map — without an account at all. Location is requested only when you use a feature that needs it, and the app works if you decline.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, no contacts, no browsing history, no background location, and no payment information. The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

Where your data is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and file storage on our behalf. Push notifications are delivered through Expo and then Apple’s or Google’s push services. These providers process data only to run the service for us.

If you sign in with Google, Kakao, or Naver, that provider tells us your email address and basic profile information so we can create your account. We do not receive your password, and we do not post anything on your behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Delete your account and it is removed immediately — see below.

Deleting your account

Open My Page in the app and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account, profile, photo, saved and visited lists, and notification settings. It happens right away and cannot be undone. You do not need to email anyone to do this.

Children

MGN RADAR is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

You can view and change your name and photo in the app, turn notifications and location off at any time in your device settings, and delete everything using the button above. If you are in a jurisdiction that grants additional rights over your data — such as a copy of it — contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app before it takes effect.

Contact
